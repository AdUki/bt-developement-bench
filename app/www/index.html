        Filter syntax
        
Terms separated by spaces, all must match; !term negates one; | is "or" inside a value.
          type:cmd|evt|acl|sco|iso|index|log|mgmt · dir:tx|rx ·
          proto:hci|l2cap|att|smp|sdp|rfcomm|avdtp|rtp|avctp|bnep|hid|sco|iso ·
          handle:11 cid:0x40 psm:25 index:0 ·
          opcode:0x0406 (a command and its Complete/Status) evt:0x13 subevt:0x02 ·
          is:err|mark|cont|lat · len>100 n<=500 lat>20 (TX → NOCP, ms) ·
          t>=12.5 t<30 (seconds since the first packet). Any other word, or a "quoted phrase",
          is searched in the summaries (case-insensitive) — slower, so on a big file the results arrive over a few seconds.
          Drag across the graph to select a time range; click it to jump there.
